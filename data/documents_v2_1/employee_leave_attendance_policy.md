Full-time employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated annual-leave entitlement based on their contracted weekly hours. Full-time annual leave accrues at 2 working days per completed month. An employee who joins after the first day of a month accrues leave for that month only if the employee works at least 15 calendar days in that month.
