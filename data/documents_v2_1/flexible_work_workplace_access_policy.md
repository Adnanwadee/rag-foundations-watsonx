## 8. Working from Outside Kuwait

Working from outside Kuwait requires separate written approval from the department head, HR, and IT Operations. A request to work from outside Kuwait must be submitted at least 15 working days before travel and must state the country, dates, reason, time zone, data-access needs, and emergency contact details. Approved outside-Kuwait work is normally limited to 10 working days in a calendar year. Outside-Kuwait work may be refused because of client-data restrictions, tax or employment concerns, sanctions risk, time-zone impact, or security controls.
